The first look

Where a recruiter's eyes actually go.

In eye-tracking work on recruiters, the first fit call took about six seconds, and CVs that passed were read for far longer. That first pass follows the same path almost every time:

  1. Name
  2. Current title and company
  3. Current start and end dates
  4. Previous title and company
  5. Previous dates
  6. Education

Source: TheLadders eye-tracking study (2012, about 30 recruiters). If any of these takes more than about two seconds to find, the CV fails the scan. Our section B tests for it.

Questions

Straight answers.

Will this "beat the ATS"?

No one can promise that, and anyone who does is selling the 75% myth. Most systems don't auto-reject; they store and search. We make sure your CV parses cleanly and turns up when a recruiter searches the job's own terms, then that it survives the first human look.

How is the score calculated?

Four sub-scores feed the signal score. ATS readability checks layout, headings and contact details. Recruiter impact checks dates, evidence in bullets, your summary, AI-styled wording and length. Job fit compares your CV with the job ad: title, key terms, years, degree, languages, certifications and location. Market fit applies the conventions of the market where the job is. Job fit counts most: a polished CV aimed at the wrong role scores low, and so does a well-aimed CV a parser can't read. Heavy use of words recruiters flag as AI-written lowers the score.

How accurate is an automated scan?

It is reliable on structure: columns, headings, dates, contact details, numbers, buzzwords and keyword match. It can't judge whether your best achievement is really your best. The $5 and $15 studios help you fix that yourself, with the score updating as you edit.

One page or two?

Under about five years of experience, one full page. Mid-career, two full pages, with page two at least two-thirds full. Never a page and a half. Gulf and academic CVs can run longer.

Should I add a photo?

It depends on the market. Leave it out for the US and UK. In the Gulf it is expected, and it is common in Lebanon, Germany and France. If you include one, place it inline in the body, not in a header or text box. The scan checks this against the market you choose.

Which files can I scan?

Text-based PDF and DOCX, or pasted text. A scanned or image-only PDF will fail the parsing check, and an ATS would struggle with it too.
